## How to Use

1. Choose a prompt that matches your task.
2. Read the instructions.
3. Replace customizable placeholders with your information.
4. Paste the prompt into your preferred AI tool.
5. Review and refine the output.

## Quality Standard

Every published prompt should have a clear purpose, useful instructions, practical examples, and documented limitations where relevant.

**Quality over quantity. Always.**
