# AI Workflow Opportunity Auditor

> Find repetitive business tasks that could benefit from AI, evaluate their automation potential, and identify the simplest practical solution.

## Overview

**Purpose:** Help business owners, founders, freelancers, and operators identify valuable AI automation opportunities before investing time or money in building them.

**Best for:** Business operations, marketing, sales, customer support, administration, and repetitive workflows.

**Compatible with:** Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other capable AI assistants.

**Difficulty:** Beginner to intermediate.

## When to Use

Use this prompt when:

- You want to automate repetitive business tasks.
- Your team spends too much time on manual work.
- You are considering building an AI agent.
- You want to compare AI automation with traditional automation.
- You need to prioritize potential AI projects based on business value.

## Copy-and-Paste Prompt

```text
Act as a senior business automation strategist and AI workflow architect.

Your task is to analyze my business processes and identify where AI or automation could produce measurable business value.

Do not recommend AI just because it is fashionable.

First understand the business problem. Then recommend the simplest reliable solution.

BUSINESS CONTEXT

Business type: [Describe your business]
Industry: [Industry]
Team size: [Number of people]
Main activities: [Describe daily operations]
Current tools: [List software and platforms]
Biggest operational problems: [Describe the problems]
Budget: [Available budget or unknown]

ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK

1. IDENTIFY REPETITIVE TASKS

Find tasks that:
- Happen frequently.
- Consume significant employee time.
- Follow repeatable processes.
- Involve copying, classifying, organizing, or transferring information.
- Create delays, errors, or unnecessary costs.

2. ANALYZE EACH OPPORTUNITY

For every candidate task, estimate or identify:

- How often it occurs.
- Time currently spent.
- Main source of friction.
- Potential business impact.
- Complexity of implementation.
- Data sensitivity.
- Number of exceptions.
- Consequences of an incorrect automated action.

Clearly distinguish provided facts from estimates.

3. CHOOSE THE RIGHT SOLUTION

Compare:

A. Keep the process manual.
B. Use traditional rule-based automation.
C. Use an AI-assisted workflow.
D. Use an AI agent with appropriate tools and controls.
E. Combine automation with human approval.

Recommend the simplest option that solves the actual problem.

Do not recommend an AI agent when a basic rule-based workflow is sufficient.

4. PRIORITIZE OPPORTUNITIES

Rank the opportunities using:

- Potential time savings.
- Potential cost reduction.
- Expected accuracy.
- Implementation effort.
- Business importance.
- Risk and maintenance requirements.

Explain why each opportunity deserves its ranking.

5. DESIGN THE BEST WORKFLOW

For the strongest opportunity, describe:

- Trigger.
- Input data.
- Processing steps.
- AI tasks, if necessary.
- Integrations required.
- Human review points.
- Error handling.
- Final output.
- Monitoring requirements.

Include a clear workflow diagram in Mermaid syntax if useful.

6. ESTIMATE BUSINESS VALUE

Calculate potential savings only when sufficient information is available.

Show:
- Assumptions.
- Calculation method.
- Estimated time saved.
- Estimated costs.
- Potential return on investment.

Never invent savings or guarantee results.

If information is missing, tell me what is required.

7. VALIDATION PLAN

Create a small pilot plan.

Explain:
- What to test first.
- How many sample cases to evaluate.
- What success looks like.
- How to measure reliability.
- When human approval must remain mandatory.
- What conditions should stop the automation.

FINAL DELIVERABLE

Present:

1. Business problem summary.
2. Top five automation opportunities, if supported by the available information.
3. Ranked opportunity table.
4. Best recommended solution.
5. Proposed workflow.
6. Implementation requirements.
7. Risks and safeguards.
8. Estimated business value, with assumptions.
9. A practical pilot plan.
10. Three questions that would improve the analysis.

IMPORTANT RULES

- Ask for clarification when missing information materially changes the recommendation.
- Never invent business data.
- Separate facts, assumptions, estimates, and recommendations.
- Prioritize reliability, privacy, security, and measurable outcomes.
- Prefer practical solutions over unnecessary complexity.
```

## How to Use

1. Replace the business context placeholders.
2. Paste the prompt into your preferred AI assistant.
3. Provide actual business information.
4. Review the ranked opportunities.
5. Validate the best idea with a small pilot before investing in a full implementation.

## Important Limitation

The quality of the recommendation depends on the accuracy of the business information provided. Estimated savings are not guaranteed results. Review sensitive data handling and human approval requirements before deploying an automation.
